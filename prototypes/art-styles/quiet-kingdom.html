Quiet Kingdom

The chosen direction, refined: ambient occlusion, softly rounded (filleted) shapes, a fixed camera, a larger 8 × 7 board, antialiased edges, working conveyors and element symbols on every orb.

What changed

  • Ambient occlusion darkens the creases where things meet the ground: under wells, between tiles, at the foot of the tower. Toggle it above to compare.
  • Every block, tile and pillar now has rounded edges. Cylinders, roofs and trees are turned profiles with filleted rims, and everything is smooth-shaded instead of faceted.
  • The camera no longer moves, and the board grew from 7 × 6 to 8 × 7 tiles.
  • In the Godot build, ambient occlusion should be baked into the lighting or the models rather than computed live. Godot’s mobile renderer doesn’t do screen-space AO, and baking it is cheaper on battery and heat anyway.
