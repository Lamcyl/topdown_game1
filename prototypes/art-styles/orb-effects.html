Orb Effects

Primary orbs stay plain Glowing Cores. Mixed orbs (secondary elements) and doubled orbs (the same element fused twice) get an animated effect around the orb, so you can tell at a glance that they’re stronger. Here are five ways to animate it. Each one is built on the Glowing Core orb with its dark outline symbol.

Back row, doubled: 🔥 Fire ×2 · 💧 Water ×2 · 🪨 Earth ×2. Front row, mixed: ☁️ Steam (fire + water) · 🌋 Magma (fire + earth) · 🟫 Mud (water + earth).
